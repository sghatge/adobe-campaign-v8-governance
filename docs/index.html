What it demonstrates

Governance controls, made inspectable.

A practical portfolio project showing how deterministic controls and human review can work together.

01 / DECISION ENGINE

Deterministic campaign QA

Mandatory controls evaluate synthetic campaign inputs. FAIL or UNKNOWN produces BLOCK; advisory warnings cannot override the gate.

02 / INVESTIGATION

Explainable risk guidance

A separate read-only investigation layer explains findings and suggests evidence for a human reviewer. It cannot change the decision.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Human review and audit

Reviewer actions and assessment events are recorded in a local hash-chained demonstration ledger.

Architecture boundary

Clear separation of authority.

Deterministic QA owns APPROVE / BLOCK. Investigation is advisory. Human review records accountability. No production campaign execution, release integration, or customer data is involved.

The current audit ledger uses local JSON storage and SHA-256 hash chaining. It is tamper-evident for demonstration purposes, not a production immutable ledger or durable multi-user database.
